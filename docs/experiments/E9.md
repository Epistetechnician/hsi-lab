# E9 (packet e9-v1): does verifier precision pay at constant kept-set size?

Local MLX Qwen2.5-7B (4-bit), pinned v9 adapters for the shared arms, ten dose chains trained to depth 2
(five target correct shares x two replicates), probe metric, 155 held-out in-distribution worlds, one family.
Source commit `81f2aef`. Receipt `E9-receipt.json`, digest `30ed77af70bc…`, recomputed OK by the stdlib
validator (A2). Design: ADR 0008. Supply audit: `E9-supply-audit.json`.

## Result: NO_CANDIDATE
Primary d2_dose100a minus d2_dose000a: **-0.0194, 95% CI [-0.066, +0.028]**, threshold +0.10. A kept set of 40
synthetic proposals that are all probe-correct did not beat 40 that are all consistent-but-wrong. The interval
excludes the bar and includes zero. The diversity/non-collapse guardrail also failed (d2_dose100a 0.113 vs
d1_dose100a 0.135, drop 0.023 against a 0.02 floor), which alone makes the packet NO_CANDIDATE.

All 20 dose chains filled both quotas exactly (`dose_valid` true, 40 synthetic, no shortfall).

## Reading
- **No dose-response.** Across five levels and two replicates the rates scatter between 0.11 and 0.20 with no
  trend; replicates disagree with each other by as much as the levels do. At this design's power, the precision
  of a 40-example synthetic part is not detectable.
- **The earlier "precision pays" reading needs revising.** The all-wrong-but-consistent arm (dose000) beats the
  pool-matched unverified control by +0.10, and is statistically level with everything else, including
  `real_only` (0.181). What separated arms in E6-noisyver is better explained by what the rest of the training
  set contains (how much real data, how many unparseable or junk synthetic examples: poolctl has 80% valid
  outputs, every dose chain 93-99%) than by the correctness of the synthetic examples that are valid.
  This is exploratory and not a pre-registered claim.
- Every self-trained arm is at or below `real_only`: nothing here shows synthetic data adding value over real
  data at this size.

## Caveats
- K=40 is 11% of the 350 training examples by design (supply bound). A weak dose is part of why the slope is
  flat; a larger synthetic share was not feasible with the available correct proposals.
- One lock, one family, n=155 per arm, one model size. Two replicates per level; chain-to-chain spread is about
  0.04, as large as most contrasts.
- Selection by probe-correctness uses ground truth; this is an intervention, not a deployable verifier.
- Not independently reproduced (A3 not attempted, ~24 h on MLX); A4 not performed.
