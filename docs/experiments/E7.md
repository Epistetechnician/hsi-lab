Packet: `packets/e7-v1.frozen.json`. Streams of context-tagged worlds; only the last phase is
scored and every arm gets 4 fresh transitions there (same total exploration for all arms).
- relational_return: contexts A,B,C,D,A — capacity 2 forces A out of the active set.

logged RelationEvent with a source id.
