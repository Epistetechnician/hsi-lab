# E6 — recursive synthesis with and without original replay

Packet: `packets/e6-v1.frozen.json`. Generation g trains a fresh rank-2 adapter on pseudo-labels
chosen by the generation g−1 model (generation 0 is the frozen base) for unlabeled tune-world
samples; no verification is applied (that is the point). Replay arms replace half of the synthetic
set with original labeled fit-world data (equal total example count). Treatment d3_replay vs
baseline d3_no_replay; guardrails: normalized entropy of generated-task signatures at every
replay depth ≥ 0.5 × the depth-1 value (pilot floor), and d3_replay not below d1_replay by more
than 2 points.

## Result: INCONCLUSIVE (receipt `E6-receipt.json`)

d3_replay − d3_no_replay = +0.031, 95% CI [0.003, 0.059], straddling the +0.05 threshold.
Pooled held-out success: d1 replay 0.450, d3 replay 0.450, d3 no-replay 0.419. Guardrails hold
(min replay diversity 0.352 vs floor 0.268; no d3-vs-d1 drop).

No collapse was observed in three generations: restricting pseudo-labels to hypotheses consistent
with the evidence appears to anchor self-training, and diversity stayed flat (≈0.53 without
replay). So this setup neither demonstrates collapse nor a replay benefit. Diversity is measured on
the synthetic portion only. Deeper recursion or an unconstrained generator would be a new packet.

Nonclaims: pure-Python prior, three generations, one seed.
